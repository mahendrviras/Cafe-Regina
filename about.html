How It All Started

Two Baristas, One Dream

Maria and James Harlow met at a barista competition in Seattle in 2008. Both fiercely passionate about specialty coffee and even more passionate about the people who drink it, they knew they wanted to build something together.

In 2010, they signed a lease on a 900-square-foot space on SE Hawthorne Blvd — Portland's vibrant, eclectic heart — and opened Cafe Regina with $40,000 in savings, 12 secondhand chairs, and an unshakeable belief that a great cup of coffee can make someone's day a little better.

Fifteen years later, that same belief is the foundation of everything we do. We've expanded the space, grown the team, and added food to our menu — but the soul of Cafe Regina is exactly what it was on day one: people first, coffee always.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Every decision we make — from sourcing beans to hiring team members — runs through these core commitments.

🌱

Sustainable Sourcing

We buy directly from small-farm cooperatives in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, paying above fair-trade prices and visiting annually.

🤝

Community Roots

10% of our net profit goes to local nonprofits serving food security and youth education in Portland each year.

♻️

Zero-Waste Ambition

Compostable packaging, on-site composting, and a bring-your-own-cup discount of $0.25. We're on a journey to zero waste by 2027.

🎓

Continuous Learning

Every team member receives SCA barista training and we host free monthly coffee education workshops open to the public.

🌈

Inclusive Space

Cafe Regina is a safe, welcoming space for everyone. We actively foster diversity, equity, and inclusion in our team and community.

❤️

Genuine Hospitality

We remember your name, your order, and your story. That's not a policy — it's just who we are.
