Our Story

More Than Coffee — It's a Community

Cafe Regina was born from a simple dream: a place where every person who walks through the door feels instantly at home.

Founded in 2010 by barista duo Maria & James Harlow in the heart of Portland, Oregon, we've grown from a tiny corner shop to a beloved neighborhood institution — but our soul hasn't changed one bit.

We partner with small farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala to bring you beans with a story. Every roast, every latte, every croissant is made with care and intention.
